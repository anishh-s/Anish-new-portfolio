About

The person behind the pixels.

Computer science by degree, design by instinct — here’s how the two ended up in the same sentence.

Bengaluru, India
“I focus on user-friendly design, clean code practices, and applying real design principles to build interfaces that actually work.”

I’m a Computer Science Engineering graduate from Jain University, Bengaluru, with a strong focus on UI/UX design and front-end development. My technical background means I think beyond pixels — considering structure, responsiveness, interactions, and edge cases before an interface is built.

I’ve worked across both sides of the process, from redesigning real-world interfaces in Figma to translating designs into functional front-end experiences. I enjoy exploring new tools, frameworks, and ideas that help me create better digital experiences.

I’m particularly interested in the space where design, technology, and problem-solving meet — creating products that look intentional, feel intuitive, and work seamlessly.
